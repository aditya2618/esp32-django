📖 How to Connect Your ESP32 to Computer

Step 1: Get the Right Cable

• Use a micro-USB or USB-C cable (depending on your ESP32 model)
• Make sure it's a data cable, not just a charging cable

Step 2: Connect to Computer

• Plug the USB cable into your ESP32 board
• Plug the other end into your computer's USB port
• You should see a small LED light up on the ESP32

Step 3: Install Drivers (Windows Only)

If Windows doesn't automatically detect the device, install the appropriate driver:

  • CP2102/CP210x chip: Download from Silicon Labs
  • CH340/CH341 chip: Download from WCH

Step 4: Verify Connection

• Open Device Manager (Windows Key + X → Device Manager)
• Expand "Ports (COM & LPT)"
• You should see something like: USB-SERIAL CH340 (COM3) or Silicon Labs CP210x (COM4)

Step 5: Check Status Above

• The connection indicator above should turn 🟢 GREEN when ESP32 is detected
• If it's 🔴 RED, try unplugging and replugging the USB cable

💡 Troubleshooting:
• Try a different USB cable
• Try a different USB port on your computer
• Make sure the cable supports data transfer (not just charging)
• Restart your computer after installing drivers
